**ConstructPro** is a mobile-first, offline-capable Construction Field Management system designed to digitize and streamline daily site operations. It replaces WhatsApp updates, phone calls, and paper registers with a structured platform for managing Daily Progress Reports (DPRs), attendance, material tracking, and invoicing. The app enables real-time data capture from construction sites—even in low-connectivity environments—with automatic sync to a centralized dashboard for management visibility. By bringing transparency, accountability, and data-driven insights to site operations, ConstructPro improves efficiency, reduces delays, and minimizes material and cost leakages.
